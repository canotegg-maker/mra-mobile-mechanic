What we do

Mobile Auto Repairs, Diagnostics & Roadside Assistance

Diagnostics & Check Engine Lights

Check engine light on, running rough, or having trouble starting? We investigate fault codes, symptoms, and vehicle data to help identify the cause and explain the next steps before replacing parts. Additional testing may be needed depending on the problem. Explore our diagnostic services

Brakes & Suspension

We replace brake pads and rotors and service selected suspension components and wheel bearings. If you notice grinding, vibration, clunking, or changes in how your vehicle handles, contact us to discuss an inspection. Learn about brake repair · Explore suspension services

Batteries, Alternators & Starters

We diagnose starting and charging problems and replace batteries, alternators, and starters. Battery registration is available for supported vehicles when required. Battery replacement · Alternator replacement · Starter replacement

Tune-Ups & Routine Maintenance

Keep your vehicle running smoothly with oil changes, spark plugs, air filters, serpentine belts, and other maintenance based on your vehicle’s needs. We also offer throttle-body cleaning when appropriate. Explore tune-up services · Mobile mechanic services

Cooling Systems, A/C & Minor Electrical Repairs

We service water pumps, thermostats, radiators, cooling fans, and selected cooling-system components. We also offer A/C diagnostics and service, along with minor electrical troubleshooting. Contact us with your vehicle details and symptoms to discuss the work needed. Cooling-system repair · A/C service · Minor electrical repair

Roadside Assistance

Stranded with a dead battery or flat tire? We offer jump starts, battery replacement, spare-tire installation, and tire plugs when the tire is suitable for repair. Contact us with your location and situation to check availability. View roadside assistance services

Meet the owner

Get to know the owner of MRA

My name is Gilberto Miguel Rufat Alvarez, founder and owner of MRA Mobile Mechanic & Roadside Assistance.

My passion for the automotive industry began years ago, and I have been professionally working on vehicles since 2017. I graduated from Palm Beach State College in Lake Worth, Florida, where I earned my Automotive Technology certificate. Throughout my career, I gained valuable experience working in both used car dealerships and new car dealerships, allowing me to develop the skills and confidence to diagnose and repair a wide variety of vehicles.

In 2020, I decided to pursue my dream of building a company that puts customers first. That is how MRA Mobile Mechanic & Roadside Assistance was born.

Today, with over 9 years of hands-on industry experience, my mission remains simple: provide dependable, honest, and professional mobile automotive service that customers can trust. Instead of wasting your time at a repair shop or paying for expensive towing, I bring the shop directly to you.

Every repair is performed with attention to detail, using proper diagnostic procedures and quality workmanship. I take pride in doing the job right the first time and standing behind every service I provide.

At MRA, we don't cut corners; we do it right.
