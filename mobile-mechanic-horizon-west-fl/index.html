From request to repair

How the mobile appointment works

  1. Contact MRA Call or text the vehicle details, symptoms, service location, and desired timing.
  2. Confirm the appointment. We discuss whether the repair suits mobile service, travel and availability, and any booking fee or deposit before you commit.
  3. Review the findings. At the vehicle, findings and any additional testing or work are explained. No repair or specialized testing proceeds without customer approval.

A safe, accessible parking spot is needed for the agreed work. If the vehicle is unsafe to drive, mention that when you call and ask whether the concern fits roadside assistance.
