Describe the road behavior

Small details can distinguish different concerns

Mention whether a clunk occurs over one wheel or both, if the steering feels loose or pulls, whether the vehicle keeps bouncing after a bump, and if tire wear looks uneven. Note when the problem started and any recent impact or repair. These clues help plan an inspection but cannot identify a failed component without checking the vehicle.

Suspension components may need the vehicle lifted and securely supported. A safe, level surface and suitable access are essential; not every mobile location or repair is appropriate. MRA will first confirm the requested work, travel, appointment availability, and what equipment and setting the job requires.

Before the appointment

Help us understand the handling change

Noise and conditions

Record when the sound happens: turning, braking, bumps, or at a particular speed.

Ride and tire clues

Share changes in ride height, bouncing, steering, or visible tire wear.

Safety matters

If control feels compromised, do not continue driving; call to discuss the safest next step.

How to request service

Inspection first, repair plan second

  1. Send the vehicle information. Include year, make, model, symptoms, and exact location.
  2. Confirm access and scope. Discuss whether the surface and requested inspection are suitable, along with travel and timing.
  3. Review the estimate. Any repair recommendations, parts, and additional work are discussed for approval before proceeding.

Mobile appointment availability and repair suitability depend on confirmed details. Review any Square booking fee or deposit terms before scheduling.
