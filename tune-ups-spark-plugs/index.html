Maintenance with context

Not every misfire calls for spark plugs

Tell us whether the engine shakes at idle, hesitates under load, has a warning light, or has difficulty starting. Include the mileage if known, maintenance records, and any recent ignition or engine work. A scan or additional tests may be appropriate before deciding whether plugs, ignition components, or another repair is needed.

Spark plug access, required components, and service intervals vary by engine. M.R.A. will review the specific vehicle and intended scope first; tune-up work does not mean every ignition or fuel-system component is automatically replaced. Work and any parts must be agreed upon after requirements are checked.
