Help while you are visiting

When a car problem interrupts your stay

A dead battery, warning light or other vehicle problem can disrupt a planned day or make it hard to leave your lodging. If your vehicle is parked at a hotel, resort, vacation rental or other property around Kissimmee 34747 and nearby service areas, call MRA with the exact location and symptoms. We can discuss whether the concern is suitable for mobile service and whether travel is available.

MRA is an independent mobile mechanic and roadside-assistance provider. MRA is not affiliated with, endorsed by or an official service provider for Disney, any hotel or resort, or any vacation-rental company. The property owner or manager must permit outside vehicle service. You must have a safe, accessible place for work; service cannot be promised in a restricted lot, garage or other location where repairs are prohibited.

Kissimmee 34747 is MRA's central service area. Other nearby locations may be considered depending on distance and availability, including Four Corners, Celebration and Horizon West.

What to do next

Share the location and vehicle details

  1. Check property rules. Ask the hotel, resort, rental host or property manager whether an outside mechanic may service a vehicle there.
  2. Contact MRA. Share the property's address or name, your exact parking location, vehicle year/make/model, symptoms and whether the vehicle can be driven.
  3. Confirm the appointment. MRA will discuss travel, service fit, availability and any applicable fee or deposit. Do not assume a dispatch or arrival time until confirmed.
  4. Arrange a safe work area. The vehicle must be accessible in a place where service is permitted. Findings and proposed additional work are reviewed for approval.
