Roadside Assistance in Kissimmee, FL

A breakdown can leave you unsure what help to request. Call MRA with your exact location, vehicle, and current situation to ask about roadside support. Travel distance, safe access, service type, and availability must be confirmed before dispatch or appointment.

First, share your situation

Location and safety come before the repair

Tell us whether the vehicle is in a parking lot, at home, or stopped roadside; provide a nearby address or clear location details. Mention traffic exposure, visibility, passengers, and any immediate hazard. If you are in danger, move to a safe place and contact emergency services as appropriate; roadside service is not an emergency-response service.

MRA lists support such as jump starts, battery help, lockouts, flat tires, and fuel delivery. The exact request, vehicle, equipment needs, and access are reviewed first. Do not assume a specific repair, tire, fuel, or other item is available until arrangements are confirmed.

Common roadside requests

Explain what has stopped you

No-start or weak battery

Describe the lights and sounds when starting. See battery service or starting-system checks.

Flat tire or lockout

Share vehicle position, wheel access, and any safety constraints at the scene.

Mechanical warning

If a warning or new symptom appeared, describe it before attempting to drive. See diagnostic service.

Request roadside help

Confirm the support and arrival details

  1. Call with your location. Provide vehicle details, the problem, landmarks or address, and safe contact information.
  2. Confirm service and travel. Discuss whether the requested help is available for that vehicle and location, along with timing and any estimate.
  3. Stay in a safe place. Follow local road-safety guidance while waiting and update MRA if your location changes.

Roadside availability and travel are confirmed individually; a request does not guarantee dispatch or a specific arrival time. Square booking terms apply to appointments booked online.
