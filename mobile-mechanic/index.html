At your vehicle

Start with the symptoms, not a guessed repair

A warning light, new noise, rough running, or a vehicle that will not start can have several causes. A useful first conversation includes the year, make, and model, when the symptom began, any recent work, warning messages, and whether the vehicle can be moved safely. Those details help determine whether on-site diagnosis or another next step makes sense.

Mobile work needs a safe, accessible place to inspect the vehicle. MRA is a service-area business, not a customer-facing repair shop. Travel distance, appointment timing, and the work that can be done at the location are confirmed individually; not every repair is appropriate for a roadside or parking-lot setting.

Appointment workflow

What to expect

  1. Send the basics. Provide the vehicle details, symptoms, location, and timing needs by phone or text.
  2. Confirm the visit. Discuss whether the requested work is suitable for mobile service, travel, availability, and the estimate before scheduling.
  3. Review findings. At the appointment, agree on the diagnostic or repair scope before additional work proceeds.

An appointment request is not confirmation of travel or a repair. Square booking terms, including any pre-booking fee or deposit, should be reviewed before confirming there.
