Confirm the source

A discharged battery may have an underlying cause

Let us know if the vehicle cranks slowly, has no electrical power, or was recently jump-started. Mention how long it sat, battery age if known, any warning lights, and whether the issue returned after driving. A battery test and review of charging behavior can help separate a weak battery from another starting or charging concern.

Battery type and fitment vary by vehicle, and a replacement is not assumed before checking requirements. If the battery is difficult to access or the vehicle is in an unsafe position, the requested work may not suit a mobile visit. Ask about the specific car and location before arranging service.

Before contacting

Have these details ready

Vehicle fitment

Provide year, make, model, engine if known, and any start-stop or battery configuration information.

Power symptoms

Note whether lights work, the engine cranks, or a jump start temporarily changes the situation.

Charging follow-up

If a new battery loses charge, ask about an alternator and charging-system assessment.

How to arrange service

Test, confirm fit, then decide

  1. Describe the no-start. Share vehicle details, symptom history, and exact location.
  2. Confirm availability. Discuss travel, access, diagnostic options, battery specifications, and estimate before scheduling.
  3. Review the result. Agree on testing and any replacement scope; ask about disposal and applicable arrangements when discussing the quote.

Parts availability and appointment timing vary and must be confirmed. Review Square booking terms, including any fee or deposit, before confirming an appointment.
