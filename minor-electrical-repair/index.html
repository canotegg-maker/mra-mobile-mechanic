Electrical faults take testing

Intermittent does not mean unpredictable forever

Describe which lamp, switch, outlet, sensor, or accessory is affected and whether the fault changes with rain, vibration, engine start, or vehicle movement. Note warning messages and any recent battery or wiring work. A symptom may be caused by a fuse, connection, wiring, control, or another system; replacement should follow checks, not guesswork.

Some electrical problems require extensive circuit tracing, specialty equipment, or disassembly that does not suit a mobile visit. MRA will review the vehicle, symptom, and location to confirm a reasonable initial scope; further testing and any repair are discussed separately.

Appointment process

Define a practical first test

  1. Share observations. Send vehicle details, location, affected components, and conditions when the fault occurs.
  2. Confirm diagnostic scope. Discuss access, travel, availability, and whether the initial checks can be performed on site.
  3. Review next steps. Agree on findings and a separate estimate before any additional repair work proceeds.

A mobile visit is not confirmation that every electrical fault can be completed on site. Check Square terms and any fee or deposit before confirming a booking.
