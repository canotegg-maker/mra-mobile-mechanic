Separate the possible causes

What happens when you attempt to start?

Tell us whether the engine turns slowly, makes one click, clicks repeatedly, or does not respond. Mention whether dashboard lights dim, whether a jump changes anything, and if the issue is intermittent or followed recent work. These observations help guide initial checks but do not confirm a failed starter on their own.

Starter access can differ significantly across vehicles. Before scheduling, share the year, make, model, engine information if known, and exact parking location. The mobile visit, testing, part requirements, travel, and timing must be confirmed; some repairs may not fit the available access or setting.

Before a starter job

Confirm access, test results, and scope

  1. Send the symptoms. Include vehicle information, location, and what you hear or see during a start attempt.
  2. Check mobile access. Discuss whether the car is safely accessible, whether testing is suitable, and confirm travel and availability.
  3. Approve the plan. Review test findings, estimate, and any required part arrangements before repair work proceeds.

Do not assume a starter replacement is needed before diagnosis. Square appointment terms and any required pre-booking fee or deposit appear during booking.
