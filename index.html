Meet the owner

Get to know the owner of MRA Mobile Mechanic & Roadside Assistance LLC

My name is Gilberto Miguel Rufat Alvarez, also known as Cano or Miguel. I am the founder and owner of MRA Mobile Mechanic & Roadside Assistance LLC.

I have worked professionally in the automotive industry since 2017. I earned my Automotive Technology certificate from Palm Beach State College in Lake Worth, Florida, and gained hands-on experience at both new and used car dealerships. Those experiences helped me build the knowledge and skills to diagnose and repair a wide range of vehicles.

My goal is to make dependable automotive service more convenient while treating every customer with honesty and respect.

Today, I bring that same commitment to every appointment, providing mobile repairs, diagnostics, maintenance, and roadside assistance directly at your location. I take the time to explain my findings, discuss your options, and get your approval before any work begins.

As an owner and mechanic, I take pride in careful diagnostics, attention to detail, and quality workmanship. My goal is to help you understand what your vehicle needs and feel confident about the service you receive.

At MRA, we don’t cut corners, we do it right.
